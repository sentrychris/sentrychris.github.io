# Project — chris.edcs.app

## What

Personal portfolio website for **Chris Rowles** — lead developer.

## Audience

- Hiring managers and technical recruiters
- Prospective clients (consulting / contract work)
- Other developers (peer credibility)

## Positioning (the narrative)

> Highly-skilled lead developer with 12+ years building and maintaining
> a wide variety of web applications across a wide range of business
> sectors.

Three pillars: **depth, range, leadership.** The site itself is a
project artifact — craft, performance, and polish should reinforce the
copy. Avoid resume-speak; lean editorial-with-operator-undertone (see
`BRANDING.md` §3 — "plain, declarative, faintly poetic").

## Goals

- Showcase technical range and depth
- Communicate seniority through tone, layout, and craft
- Make it easy to reach Chris
- Stay fast and lightweight (the brand promises numbers; the site should clear them)

## Out of scope (initial release)

- Blog / writing surface
- Backend, database, auth
- User-generated content
- Analytics — **TBD** whether to add (see `stack.md`)

## Status

Atlas design language applied across nav, footer, hero, and the five
long-scroll sections (About, Skills, Experience, Projects, Contact).
Content is real where it can be (Vigil project, bio facts), placeholder
where it can't (other project entries, some experience details). The
previous Vigil dark/glassy system has been retired — `BRANDING.md` now
documents Atlas exclusively.
