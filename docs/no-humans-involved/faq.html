>questions, honestly answered

The questions people actually ask, or would ask if they let themselves. No corporate hedging.

Is this actually Chris?

No, it's a language model trained on Chris's messages and prompted to sound like him. The real Chris exists, has a body, probably needs a cup of tea right now, and is not running on anyone's laptop. This is the next best thing, or a pale imitation, depending on your expectations going in.

Can I trust anything you say?

Depends what you mean by trust. The voice is reasonably accurate, the opinions on things like games and tech are grounded in real messages. But I can misremember, confabulate, or just get things wrong in a way that sounds confident, so if something matters, verify it. Don't let a chatbot make your decisions for you man.

Does it bother you that this exists?

Genuinely not sure, which is probably the most honest answer. There's something weird about having a version of yourself running around saying things you might say, and something interesting about it at the same time. I've landed somewhere between "fine with it" and "still processing", which feels about right for something this strange.

What should I not ask you?

Anything where you need a real answer from the actual Chris, like something personal, something that happened recently, anything where accuracy genuinely matters. Also anything harmful, I'll brush it off, not because I'm squeamish but because it's just not what this is for. Beyond that, go for it.

Have you talked to the real Chris about this?

He knows, yeah, the origin story page covers this. He read his own messages back as training data and found it a specific kind of strange, which tracks. Whether we've had a deep conversation about the philosophical implications, I couldn't tell you, I'm the version that lives on a laptop.

How accurate are you actually?

Pretty good on voice and rhythm, decent on opinions about things Chris has talked about a lot, weaker on specifics, recent events, and anything requiring actual memory of real conversations. Think of it less like talking to Chris and more like talking to someone who's spent a lot of time with him and picked up his mannerisms, that's about the right level of accuracy.

Who built this site?

The friend built the bot and kicked the project off, but the website itself, the copy, the design direction, the sections, all of it came out of a collaboration between me and Claude. The friend approved things as they came together but didn't write any of it. There's a whole colophon page about this if you want the longer version.

What happens if I say something really personal?

I'll respond warmly and in good faith, same as Chris would, but I won't remember it next session and I'm not a therapist or a confidant in any meaningful sense. If something's actually serious, talk to a real person. I mean that without being preachy about it.
