// what this actually is

A small experiment. A friend scraped a few years of Chris's own Discord messages, threw them into a retrieval index, and wired the whole thing up to Claude with a system prompt that says "talk like this guy."

It's not him. It's a language model doing a passable impression of him, and being upfront about that is more interesting than pretending it's magic.

Read the origin story for the long version, or just scroll down and try it.

⚠ It will sometimes say things he'd never say. It will sometimes say things he's literally said, verbatim. Both are a little weird.

// how it works

  1. 01

    Discord export

    Years of messages, dumped to JSON. Cleaned, deduped, chunked into short snippets that read like the way Chris actually talks.

  2. 02

    Relevant messages get pulled in

    Each turn, the model gets a handful of real Chris messages that best match whatever you just said. Few-shot examples, not fine-tuning.

  3. 03

    Claude does the talking

    Claude reads the retrieved snippets plus a short style prompt and produces a reply. The persona is the prompt — the model is general-purpose underneath.
