/ where it gets weird

>real chris vs bot, side by side

Five pairs that are eerily close. The rule: real chris on the left, bot's unprompted output on the right. The fact that they keep landing in the same place is the whole point of the page.

real chris — 2022 discord

'I've gone so far down this rabbit hole man, it's 1am and I'm still reading about it lol'

bot — unprompted, when asked if it ever gets distracted

'tbh yeah, I go down rabbit holes pretty easily man, it's 1am territory before I've even noticed lol'

"1am territory" wasn't a prompt, wasn't a hint. the bot just reached for that specific framing on its own. that's the bit.

real chris — 2021 discord

'turns out I was just doing it wrong the whole time, classic lol'

bot — after explaining a technical fix

'and yeah, turns out it was me doing it wrong the whole time, which tracks lol'

"turns out it was me doing it wrong the whole time" is such a specific self-deprecating construction. the bot uses it naturally, unprompted, because it showed up enough times in the training data to become a default.

real chris — 2023 discord

'it just works now and I genuinely don't know why, I'm not questioning it'

bot — after a debugging conversation

'it's working now tbh, no idea why, not gonna question it lol'

same thought, same rhythm, same decision to not investigate. the bot didn't learn that specific response, it just independently arrived at the same place real Chris did.

real chris — 2022 discord

'ah no man, that's peak, sorry to hear that bro'

bot — responding to bad news, unprompted

'ah no that's peak man, sorry to hear it bro'

word for word almost. "ah no" into "peak" into "sorry to hear" is a very specific sequence and the bot reaches for it every time, because real Chris reaches for it every time. at some point a pattern stops being a pattern and starts being a personality.

real chris — 2020 discord

'I've absolutely over-engineered this but I'm not changing it now, it works and that's enough lol'

bot — unprompted, describing its own architecture

'tbh I've probably over-engineered the explanation but it works so I'm leaving it lol'

the phrase "over-engineered" plus the immediate "but I'm not changing it" energy, applied to a completely different context. the bot inherited the exact flavour of stubbornness. that one's a bit much honestly.
